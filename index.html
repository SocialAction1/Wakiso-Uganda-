ABOUT US

Who We Are

Social Action Uganda is a community-based organization operating in Wakiso District to support vulnerable people through child protection, human rights advocacy, charity activities, and community empowerment.

We believe every person deserves dignity, safety, and fair opportunities. Through community engagement and local partnerships, we work to create lasting impact in education, livelihoods, and social protection.

PROTECTION

End Child Marriage

Child marriage denies young people their rights and futures. We work to protect and empower girls and boys through education, awareness campaigns, and community engagement that create lasting change in Wakiso District.

Community & Emergency Support

We provide charity and humanitarian support to vulnerable communities in times of need. We distribute food, clothing, and essential supplies to families affected by disaster and hardship.

ADVOCACY

Human Rights Advocacy

We advocate for the protection of human rights and dignity for all community members in Wakiso District. Through legal connections, awareness campaigns, and direct intervention, we support survivors and promote access to justice for vulnerable people.
